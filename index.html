About

I've been freelancing full time since October 2025, after finishing an Advanced Diploma in Software Engineering (AI track) at Aptech. Before that, I was already building sites on the side — the freelancing just made it the whole job.

Most projects start the same way: a business owner needs a site and doesn't want to manage three different freelancers for design, code, and hosting. I handle all of it — vanilla HTML/CSS/JS on the frontend, Node.js and Express when a project needs a real backend, Supabase or PostgreSQL for data, and deployment on Vercel, Render, or GitHub Pages. If the client needs a custom domain or a proper business email address on top of the site, I set that up too.

I like this end-to-end shape of work. It means I'm the one debugging a DNS record at 11pm and the one deciding how a signup form should feel — so nothing gets lost in a handoff.
